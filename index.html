company for you

Pricing and discounts made up a portion of each insurer’s
score, but we know that cost is the top factor for many aut
insurance shoppers. Since pricing is based on persomal
factors including your age, location, vehicle and
history and gender (in most cases), it’s important to
around for car insurance quotes.

If price is the most important factor in your search
comparing car insurance quotes can help you find the
cheapest rate. But your shopping experience will also
light on a company’s customer service standards.

To find the top car insurance companies:

  1. Visit your insurance commissioner’s website to find
    other information in your area.
  2. Ask your agent or a customer service representative any
    questions to make sure you’re getting all the coverage
    you need — and nothing you don’t want.
  3. Consider smaller insurance companies, too. Small companies
    offer rates well below competitors in Small companies many locations.
  4. If you’ve had tickets, DUIs or other violations, be sure to shop
    three years after the incident, when surcharges can drop
    ignificantly.
